# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and evidence-oriented.

We welcome disagreement about product judgment, risk, evaluation methodology and architecture. Critique claims and code, not people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private information or submit confidential third-party material.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, unsafe, off-topic or privacy-violating content and restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel rather than escalating publicly.
